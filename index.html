Charles Dickens

Today, our group will tell you about Charles Dickens. He is one of the most famous English writers in history.

Dickens was born in 1812. When he was a boy, his family became very poor. His father was sent to prison because he had no money to pay debts.

Little Charles was only 12 years old, but he had to leave school and work in a dirty factory for ten hours a day. This hardship changed his life forever!

Later, Dickens returned to school and then became a journalist. Soon, he started writing his own books. He wrote about the things he knew well: poor people, orphans, and hard life in London. His most famous book is ‘Oliver Twist’. It is a story about a poor orphan boy who lives on the dangerous streets of London. This book became incredibly popular all over the world.
